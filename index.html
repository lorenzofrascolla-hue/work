How this was made. I chose the question, took the paper through 39 versions, put it in front of four senior practitioners and a Michigan Ross professor, took its timing question to NYU Stern’s Aswath Damodaran, changed it after each critique, checked figures against primary sources where I could, and cut my own headline numbers when they were wrong. Claude models from Anthropic drafted the prose, which I directed and edited, and helped build the models. The full statement is at the end of the research page.
