I write Simancas Capital, an independent research publication on AI and energy. I wrote each of these issues myself.

Each issue is free to read on Substack. The full essays are not copied here.

The Transformer Is the Binding Constraint, Not the GPU

Issue 1. Published 29 May 2026.

Argues that high-voltage transformers, not AI chips, set the pace of the AI data center buildout, because they take years to deliver and only a few manufacturers and steel suppliers can make them.

The Bottleneck Migrated. Behind the Transformer Is the Turbine.

Issue 2. Published 5 June 2026.

Argues that once transformers are available the next limit is the gas turbine that generates the power, and inside it the single-crystal metal blades that only a few specialist foundries can cast.

Read issue 2 on Substack (opens substack.com)

The Water Is Not the Bottleneck. The Right to It Is.

Issue 3. Published 14 June 2026.

Argues that the scarce thing in data center cooling is not the water itself, which operators are engineering toward almost zero use, but senior legal rights to water in the regions where data centers are built.
